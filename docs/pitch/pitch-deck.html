Bond Market

A prediction market where the larger bond decides the result.
No oracle. No admin.

Live on Portaldot V3 testnet (chain ID 420420777)
github.com/QuyTu9868/bond-market-portaldot

1 / The problem

A prediction market is only as honest
as whoever reports the result

A blockchain cannot know "did it rain tomorrow". Someone has to tell it. Today there are two ways, and both have a weak point.

Pay for an oracle

An external data service reports the result. It costs money, adds a third party, and a brand new chain usually has none yet.

Trust one person

The market creator reports, and a contract owner judges disputes. If that person cheats or disappears, everyone loses.

2 / Our solution

Let money decide: a bond war

1. BetAnyone bets Yes or No with native tPOT before the deadline.
→
2. BondAfter the deadline, anyone posts a bond on Yes or No. The first bond opens a window.
→
3. OutbidDuring the window anyone can add bond to either side. A wrong claim can be outbid.
→
4. SettleLarger total bond wins. Winners share the bets and the losing bonds.

Worked example

Ann bets 1 on Yes, Ben bets 3 on No. Chi bonds 5 on Yes, Dan bonds 2 on No. Yes wins: Ann receives 4, Chi receives 7, Ben and Dan receive nothing.

Safety nets

Bonds tie, or nobody bonds for 7 days: everyone is refunded. The contract has only 5 write functions. No owner, no admin, no fee.

4 / Why Portaldot

A new chain needs apps that work
without extra infrastructure

No oracle needed

The result comes from bonds on chain, so the app works on Portaldot from day one, with nothing to integrate.

Solidity just works

Standard Solidity and Hardhat deploy and run on Portaldot V3 through its EVM layer (pallet_revive). We shipped and ran the full flow there.

Native token only

Bets and bonds use the chain's own tPOT. No token approvals, no bridge, a simple first experience for new users.

What we learned building on it: deploying costs a storage deposit, about 530 tPOT for our 6.7 KB contract. On Portaldot, contract size matters, and we plan to shrink ours. This is useful guidance for other builders in the ecosystem.

Honest limits today

A very rich actor could win a bond war, but must out-bond everyone else combined. The resolution window is fixed, so a late large bond leaves little time to answer. Pool-sharing payouts, no live price like Polymarket. Not audited.
